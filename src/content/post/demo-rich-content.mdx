## States

A general qubit state is

$$
|\psi\rangle = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}\,|0\rangle + e^{i\varphi}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\,|1\rangle \label{eq:qubit}
$$

where measuring gives $|0\rangle$ with probability $\cos^2(\theta/2)$, as <Ref to="fig:probabilities" /> shows.
The angles in <Ref to="eq:qubit" /> place the state on the Bloch sphere [@nielsen2010].

<Definition title="Qubit" id="def:qubit">
  A **qubit** is a unit vector in $\mathbb{C}^2$, up to a global phase.
</Definition>

## No cloning

<Lemma id="lem:inner">
  Unitary maps preserve inner products.
</Lemma>

<Theorem title="No-cloning" id="thm:no-cloning">
  No unitary $U$ satisfies $U|\psi\rangle|0\rangle = |\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle$ for every state $|\psi\rangle$ [@wootters1982].
</Theorem>

<Proof>
  Copying $|\psi\rangle$ and $|\phi\rangle$ would give $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle = \langle\psi|\phi\rangle^2$ by <Ref to="lem:inner" />,
  so the overlap is 0 or 1: only identical or orthogonal states could be copied.
</Proof>

<Callout type="tip" title="Why it matters">
  <Ref to="thm:no-cloning" /> is why quantum error correction can't simply keep backup copies of a qubit.[^qec]
</Callout>

[^qec]: It spreads the information over entangled qubits instead.
